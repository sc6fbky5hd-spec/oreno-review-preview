03 / HOW THE ORDER WORKS

Support matters.
So does conviction.

The community order combines how strongly people recommend a pick with how many independent people support it. A neutral starting prior keeps a single enthusiastic answer from dominating a mature list.

Results are labeled early, building, or established as evidence grows. The live product shows real counts. This preview labels sample data.
